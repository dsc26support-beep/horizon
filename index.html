Our place

Where the day meets the lagoon.

Horizon's Bar sits on Betio, at the western tip of South Tarawa, looking out over the lagoon. Come by after work, after a swim, or whenever you need a cold drink and a view of the sea.

Locals, visitors, old friends and new ones all end up at the same tables when the sky turns orange. Pull up a chair. The horizon is the main event.

UTC+12 One of the first time zones to see each new day
