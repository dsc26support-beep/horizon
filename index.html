About us

Where the day meets the lagoon.

Horizon's Bar sits on Betio, at the western tip of South Tarawa, looking out over the lagoon. It's an easy, welcoming place to spend the evening, whether you're bringing the kids, meeting friends after work or visiting Kiribati for the first time.

Find a table, order something cold and watch the sky change colour. Everyone is welcome at Horizon's.

UTC+12 One of the first time zones to see each new day
1.3°N Close to the equator, so it's warm all year
West Betio faces west, so you get the sunset over open water
