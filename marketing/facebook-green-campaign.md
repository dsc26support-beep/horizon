# Green Hour: Facebook campaign for Horizon's Bar

**Idea:** a twist on "golden hour". Same sunset and cold drinks, with less plastic on the lagoon.
**Length:** 2 weeks (Mon 5 Oct – Sun 18 Oct 2026)
**Goal:** more people visiting at sunset, more page followers, and a good turnout for one beach clean-up.

## Confirm before posting
| Item | Placeholder | Where it's used |
|---|---|---|
| Bring-your-own-cup discount | `[X]%` | Post 2 graphic and caption |
| Clean-up date and time | Sat 17 Oct, 4 pm | Post 3 graphic, posts 3 and 5 |
| "Sunset drink on us" for volunteers | Free drink | Post 3 |
| Ad budget | A$40 total | Boost on post 3 |

To change the graphics, edit `graphics/posts.html` and re-export, or tell Claude what to change.

## Audience
- **Locals:** South Tarawa residents aged 18–55 (Betio, Bairiki, Bikenibeu)
- **Visitors and expats:** NGO, government and aid staff, contractors, travellers
- **Message:** a relaxed bar that cares about the lagoon. Friendly, not preachy.

## Boost settings for post 3
In Meta Business Suite, open post 3 → **Boost post**:
- **Goal:** Get more engagement (or "Get more messages" if you want RSVPs in Messenger)
- **Audience:** create a new one
  - Location: *South Tarawa, Kiribati*, plus about 15 km around Betio
  - Age: 18–55
  - Interests: optional, e.g. beaches, environmentalism, nightlife
- **Budget:** A$5/day × 8 days = **A$40**
- **Duration:** Fri 9 Oct → Sat 17 Oct, 12 pm

## How to publish (about 10 minutes)
1. Open **business.facebook.com** → **Planner** (or Content) → **Create post**.
2. For each row in the schedule, upload the graphic, paste the caption, and choose **Schedule** with the date and time.
3. Once post 3 is scheduled, set up its boost using the settings above.
